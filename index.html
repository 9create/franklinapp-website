Questions

Frequently asked

What's the difference between the two tracks?

Junior Builders (8–12) uses simple, guided tools and is open for enrollment now. Advanced Track (13–16) tackles bigger, more ambitious projects and opens in September. Both publish a real app on Google Play Store.

Does my child need prior coding experience?

No. Both tracks are built for complete beginners and progress gradually from day one.

Why only 4 students per batch?

Small batches mean every student gets hands-on guidance and nobody falls behind, in either track.

What if a class is missed?

Makeup classes and recorded sessions are provided, so no student misses any content.
